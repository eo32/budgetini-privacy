Budgetini Support

Budgetini is a shared savings tracker for couples. If you need help, have found a bug, or want to request a feature, use the support contact address (to be published here before release) and I'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I invite my partner?
Open a budget, tap the gear icon, choose “Invite Someone,” and share the QR code or link. They open it on their device, sign in, and tap “Join.”

How do I delete my account?
Tap the profile icon at the top left of the “My Budgets” screen and choose “Delete Account.” This permanently removes your account and your savings data.

Does Budgetini connect to my bank?
No. You enter savings amounts yourself. The app never connects to a bank, card, or financial institution.
