Budgetini Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

Budgetini (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a shared savings tracker for couples. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: elmar.okanovic@hey.com.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Elmar Okanovic. Data is stored on our behalf by Supabase, Inc., our hosting and database provider, on servers located in the United States (AWS us-west-1).

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a display name. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple’s private relay address. We never receive your Apple password.
  • Content you create. The budgets you create or join, the savings amounts you log, descriptions and dates you attach to them, deposit records, and goal amounts. These are monetary amounts you type in yourself. The app does not connect to your bank, card, or any financial institution, and does not import transactions or account balances.
  • Shared-budget membership. When you invite someone to a budget or join one, the other members of that budget can see your display name and the entries and deposits you add to that budget.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs kept by Supabase (IP address, timestamps, error diagnostics) for security and reliability. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or share it with data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your device contacts, photos, or location.

Why we use it and our legal basis

We process account information and the content you create to provide the app’s core function — storing your savings entries and syncing shared budgets between members in real time. The legal basis is performance of our agreement with you (providing the service). Technical logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see below), your profile and the savings entries you created in budgets where you were the only member are permanently deleted immediately. In budgets you shared with someone else, the entries and deposits you added remain with that budget so the other member’s totals stay correct, but they are anonymized — your name is removed and shown as “Former member.” Server backups roll off within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app: tap the profile icon on the “My Budgets” screen, choose Delete Account, and confirm. This removes your login and profile, deletes budgets where you are the only member, transfers ownership of shared budgets to another member, and — if you signed in with Apple — revokes the app’s access to your Apple ID. The action cannot be undone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can exercise access and deletion directly in the app, or contact us at the email above. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in an access-controlled Postgres database with row-level security so that only members of a budget can read its contents. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
